Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I start tutoring sessions?

Most students can begin within 1-2 weeks of their initial consultation. We'll match you with the perfect tutor and schedule your first session at your convenience.

What are your tutoring rates?

Our general hourly rate ranges between £10 and £25, depending on the student's needs. We offer a 20% discount for each additional sibling after one full-paying child. We recommend 2-hour sessions for maximum efficiency.

Do you offer online tutoring sessions?

We primarily offer in-person tuition across the West Midlands, including Dudley, Birmingham, Wednesbury, Nottingham, and West Bromwich. We also offer in-home tuition subject to availability.

How do you match students with tutors?

Our tutors are carefully selected and must pass an exam at their interview to ensure the highest quality of teaching. We identify individual learning styles and tailor lessons to fit your specific needs, whether in small groups, one-on-one sessions, or in-home tuition.
